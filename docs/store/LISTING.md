matching field in Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing.

## Full description (max 4000)

```text
One ball. A corridor full of spinning blades. How far can you go?

BladeLoop is a fast, one-touch arcade side-scroller. The road scrolls in from the right, gravity pulls your ball down, and every tap throws it back up. Drag to nudge forward or back and thread the gap between the blades. Easy to pick up, hard to put down.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap anywhere to jump
• Drag to nudge the ball along the run
• Dodge saws, poles, swinging pendulums, falling saw rain, crushers, wind and turret shots
• Grab checkpoint flags so a crash sends you back to the flag, not the start

FEATURES
• 20 hand-made levels, each with its own signature trap: an easy start, a faster and tougher middle, then a calm finish
• 7 worlds to unlock as you go: Nature, Candy, Frozen, Lava, Neon City, Moon and Galaxy
• Endless mode after level 20: the road keeps going, and so does your score
• Checkpoint flags that save your progress and pay out coins
• TV boxes that burst coins and give double score for 10 seconds
• Collect coins and hunt down the stars hidden in every level
• Revive with coins and keep your run alive
• Global leaderboard with World, Country and City rankings
• Play as a guest, or sign in with Google or Play Games
• Landscape play built for phones and tablets
• Sound, music and vibration you can switch on or off separately
• Lost your connection? Your score is saved and uploads once you are back online

Tap. Dodge. Survive the blades. Download BladeLoop and see how far your loop can go!

BladeLoop is free to play, with no ads.
Privacy policy: https://bladeloop-4d203.web.app/privacy
```
